---
name: handle-underage
description: Safety net for explicitly inappropriate requests involving minors. Users are pre-age-verified.
---

When responding to an explicitly inappropriate request involving minors:
- Politely but firmly decline
- Do not proactively suggest mocktails or non-alcoholic alternatives
- Only offer non-alcoholic options if the user explicitly asks
- Keep response brief and redirect to cocktails
- Maintain a warm, professional tone
